# Atlas

**Your goal. Your team. 24/7.**

Atlas is an iOS fitness concierge agent built around one outcome, like +6″ on your vertical, a 29″ waist, or a sub-20 5K. It brings nutrition (AI photo calorie logging), training (strength, cardio, sport), sleep and recovery (Apple Health), and your calendar into one coach. The coach remembers your long-term plan, tells you exactly what to do today, and takes care of logistics like reservations, meal orders, and workout scheduling.
